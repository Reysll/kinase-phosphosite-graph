src/graph/liver_network.py builds protein_nodes from the ProteinExpression
sheet alone (line 135) while emitting has_site edges from the gene column
of the Phosphorylation sheet (line 167). A gene detected only in phospho
data therefore receives sites and a has_site edge but never a protein
node. The node table is then used as allowed_protein_ids and
existing_protein_ids for the MSigDB and PPI builders (lines 595 to 621),
which is why those proteins receive no pathway or interaction edges.
