READING THE COLUMNS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
dims_r2_gt_099   how many of the 32 kept dimensions ARE sqrt(degree),
                 over GIANT-COMPONENT nodes. The fix drives this to 0.
..._all_nodes_scope  the same count measured over ALL nodes, which
                 reads 0 under BOTH settings and so hides the defect:
                 ARPACK returns a mixture of the per-component degree
                 vectors, and a mixture does not fit sqrt(degree)
                 globally even though each piece fits it exactly.
norm_vs_degree   Spearman between a kinase's embedding-vector NORM
                 and its graph degree. This is the channel the fix
                 does NOT remove.
after_rownorm    the same dimension count after scaling every row to
                 unit length (Ng-Jordan-Weiss convention).

THE MECHANISM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L_norm u = lambda u is equivalent to L_rw (D^-1/2 u) = lambda (D^-1/2 u),
so every eigenvector of the normalized Laplacian is u = D^1/2 v for an
eigenvector v of the random-walk Laplacian. Entry i of ANY such
eigenvector carries a factor sqrt(deg_i). The null space is just the
case where v is constant and u is EXACTLY sqrt(deg), which is why
dropping it removes the literal degree dimensions. What survives is
the per-node scale: node i's embedding has norm sqrt(deg_i)*||v_i||.
Pair features [K, S, |K-S|, K*S] are built from those vectors, so a
kinase with large degree still enters the classifier with a larger
vector and keeps its global position.
