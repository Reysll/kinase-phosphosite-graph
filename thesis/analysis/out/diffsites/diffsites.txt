LIMITATIONS
================================================================================
Added after review. Full working in diffsites_limitations.txt, reproduced by
    .venv312/Scripts/python -m thesis.analysis.diffsites_limitations

1. NOT CORRECTED FOR PROTEIN ABUNDANCE.
   A phosphosite can change because the protein carrying it changed, not
   because its phosphorylation changed. These results do not separate the two.
   The ProteinExpression sheet cannot support a correction:
     - 444 rows, 91.7% of cells empty, median 0 observed values per row
     - only 2 rows have >= 6 observed values in both groups
     - only 81 of 2,973 tested sites (2.7%) have their protein in the sheet
     - only 19 of 659 shortlist sites (2.9%) do
     - 0 of 659 shortlist sites have protein data sufficient for a correction
   Its caption also describes it as proteins UNIQUE TO tumour tissue, which is
   not a matched quantitative matrix. Any site taken forward for validation
   should have its protein level checked independently.

2. THE 114 PRESENCE/ABSENCE SITES ARE CANDIDATES, NOT A STATISTICAL FINDING.
   They are selected by a detection-count filter and then tested with a
   detection-count test, so all 114 reaching significance is largely a property
   of the filter rather than evidence about each site. Report them as a
   follow-up list.

   Their batch structure was checked and is reassuring on a separate question:
     - 0 of 114 have detections confined to a single batch
     - 76 of 114 (66.7%) span 4 or more of the 6 batches
     - for 114 of 114 (100%), every batch containing a detection also had the
       opposite group well covered
   So the one-sided detection is not an artifact of a batch in which the other
   condition was simply not being measured.

3. ROUTE B BEING FULLY CONTAINED IN ROUTE A IS NEAR-AUTOMATIC.
   All 15 KSEA-significant kinases also appear in Route A. This is close to
   structural rather than corroborative: Route A collects every kinase annotated
   to any one of 1,548 significant sites and contains 109 kinases, while KSEA
   scores only kinases with at least 3 measured substrates, which are largely
   the same well-annotated kinases. Containment should NOT be presented as
   agreement between two independent methods.

4. SINGLE COHORT, NO EXTERNAL REPLICATION, UNRESOLVED PROVENANCE.
   18 against 18, no validation in a second dataset, and the originating study
   is still unidentified.

5. UNPAIRED DESIGN ON PROBABLY-PAIRED DATA.
   The layout is consistent with 18 matched pairs but the pairing is not
   recorded, so the test is unpaired. That is conservative, not anti-conservative.
