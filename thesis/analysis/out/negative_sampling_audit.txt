==============================================================================
WHAT THE CODE DOES, STATED PLAINLY
==============================================================================
  For every training site, the negative examples are the first 50
  alphabetically-ordered candidate kinases that are not known to act on
  that site. The same small set of early-alphabet kinases is therefore
  used as a negative example at nearly every site, and the large
  majority of candidates are never presented to the classifier as a
  negative at all.

  This is a defect in the training design, not in the evaluation: the
  ranking still scores all 415 candidates. Its consequence is that the
  decision boundary is fitted against an alphabetically determined
  subset of the candidate space.
